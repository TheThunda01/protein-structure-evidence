# Assumptions and their consequences

These are the material assumptions and deliberate choices in the current implementation. A check can verify a rule without establishing its scientific truth. Terms are defined in the [glossary](glossary.md); source details and access limits are in the [reference audit](reference-audit.json).

| Assumption or choice | Rationale and evidence | What is checked or remains untested | Consequence if it fails or is generalized |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The pinned records describe the intended comparison. | UniProt P00918 provides the sequence reference; RCSB 2CBA chain A and the recorded AFDB v6 model specify the coordinate sources. These are source-selection decisions, not a representative protein sample. | Eight source hashes, sequence identities and metadata are checked. Source biological correctness is not independently re-established. | Conclusions apply to this case and version only. |
| Composed residue mappings are appropriate for matched amino acids. | Author labels differ from canonical numbering; SIFTS and RCSB mappings are preferable to subtracting a constant. See Choudhary et al. (2023) and the linked source records. | Bounds, duplicates and identities are checked; ACE1 and unmodeled Ser2 do not enter the fit. | A mapping error can invalidate correspondence despite a plausible overlay. |
| Selected Cα atoms are useful backbone representatives. | This keeps the geometry small and inspectable. It omits side chains, metals, waters and most atom contacts. | 258 matched pairs are present. Coverage is reported. | Backbone similarity does not validate detailed chemistry or function. |
| One alternate position can represent each selected atom for this calculation. | Highest occupancy is the declared choice; ties prefer blank, then A, then alphabetical order. Occupancy is a model parameter, not proof of the solution-state population. | The parser checks duplicate keys and records the chosen alternate/occupancy. | Alternative conformations and their uncertainty are not propagated. |
| An equal-weight proper rigid fit answers the geometric question. | Kabsch (1976) supplies the least-squares formulation. No flexible deformation is estimated and experimental occupancy does not weight the objective. | Numerical tests cover rotations, reflections and degenerate inputs; an independent implementation has not checked this fit. | The result is conditional on correspondence, atom weights and fit set. |
| Thresholds 0, 70 and 90 are illustrative fit selections. | The confidence guide explains broad pLDDT categories. These thresholds are not tuned to this protein or calibrated as functional cutoffs. | All three fixed selections report both fit and full matched-set results. | A smaller subset RMSD is not evidence that the prediction improved. |
| Confidence fields carry the recorded AFDB meaning. | AFDB stores pLDDT in the coordinate B-factor column; its separate sidecar permits a cross-check. Experimental B values retain their different displacement-parameter meaning. | The sidecar and Cα column agree within rounding tolerance. | Neither a pLDDT value nor a B value identifies the cause of a structural difference. |
| No independent-generalization claim is possible. | 2CBA was available before AlphaFold2 training. Training/template membership for this prediction has not been audited. | Chronology is verified, but actual inclusion or exclusion is unknown. | Geometric agreement remains descriptive even if very close. |
| A coordinate difference may have several causes. | Prediction, chemical representation, crystal conditions and model uncertainty all differ; Håkansson et al. (1992) studied multiple chemical states. | No experiment or simulation separates these causes here. | The analysis cannot assign a displacement to flexibility, catalysis or error alone. |
| The export’s legacy PDB representation is adequate for inspection. | wwPDB 3.3 specifies fixed-width coordinates with three decimal places. Reusing stale coordinate-frame/tensor records could misrepresent the transform. | Unsupported records and overflow fail; every output atom is checked after rounding. | The export is not suitable evidence of crystallographic refinement or general mmCIF support. |
| The verifier’s trust anchors remain trustworthy. | Hashes detect a change relative to pinned content. Recomputing with the existing fitter checks consistency beyond editable receipt claims. | Source/algorithm fingerprints and derived atoms are checked; metadata signatures are not used. | Coordinated alteration of the trusted repository is outside this check; authorship and biological validity remain unverified. |
| The declared numerical environment is sufficiently compatible. | Python 3.12 and pinned NumPy/Matplotlib versions constrain reproducibility. Floating-point arithmetic and coordinate rounding have finite precision. | Version metadata and tolerances are recorded; strict runtime matching is optional. | Other platforms may require justified numerical tolerance; matching versions does not prove correct execution. |

The existing [methods](methods.md) and [practical-use guide](practical-use.md) define operational behavior. These assumptions do not add new experiments or change the preserved results.
